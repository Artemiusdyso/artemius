There are stories we read.

And then there are those that, little by little, seem to read us in return.

Lea and the Source Code may belong to that second category.

Before beginning this journey, we must accept a simple idea: what if what we call reality were only a tiny fraction of what truly exists? What if, behind the world our eyes perceive, behind our thoughts, memories and certainties, there were a larger, older and more mysterious structure?

A language we have forgotten.
A knowledge that cannot be passed on through words.
A code.

Human beings have always searched for answers. We look up at the stars to understand where we come from. We look deep within ourselves to discover who we are. Between these two directions — the infinitely vast and the infinitely intimate — may lie the real question: why are we here?

Lea is not simply looking for an answer.
She is looking for a door.

And perhaps that door is not somewhere in space, in a forgotten age, or even in an unreachable dimension.

Perhaps it lies where no one thinks to look: at the heart of consciousness. The journey that begins here is therefore as inward as it is outward.

It crosses worlds, mysteries and encounters, but above all it passes through the different layers of what we believe ourselves to be. Every discovery brings a new question. Every answer seems to open a deeper door. And the further Lea goes, the more she understands that some truths are not meant to be possessed. They are meant to be lived.

The “Source Code” may not be a secret formula capable of explaining the universe. It could be something else. A memory. A vibration. A frequency. An invisible architecture connecting everything to a common origin.

What if each of us carried, somewhere within, a fragment of that code?

Then perhaps our personal story is not as isolated as we think. Perhaps the encounters that transform our lives are not always accidents. Perhaps some coincidences are signs we do not yet know how to read. Perhaps the worlds we seek beyond the stars also exist deep within ourselves.

This book is an invitation.
An invitation to slow down.
To listen.
To question what we think we know.

To look differently at what surrounds us, but also at what lies within us.

Lea's story is a story of quest, discovery and awakening.

But it may also be a question addressed to each of us. What if the universe truly had a language? What if consciousness were far greater than we imagine?

And what if the secret we have spent our lives searching for were already written somewhere inside us? Would we be able to recognize it?

The journey begins now.

And perhaps, at the end of the path, when the final veil is lifted, Lea will not simply discover the Source Code.

She will discover something far more unsettling…
